Introduction

The project is designed to strengthen your ability to translate operational scenarios into clear, complete, and professional process documentation. By completing this final project, you will be able to:

Use case: Request defective part replacement
Scenario: The scenario describes a customer who returns to an auto parts store because a recently purchased part is defective. The store must verify the purchase, inspect the part, check inventory, and decide whether to issue a replacement, refund, credit, or deny the request. Your task is to document this entire process clearly through a process map and a process narrative.

Actor: Customer (Auto Parts Buyer)
Goal: Replace a defective auto part recently purchased.

Basic flow
Customer requests service
The customer contacts the auto parts store—either in person, by phone, or through an online portal—and reports that the part is defective.

Customer provides purchase details
The customer supplies their order number or receipt, enabling the store system or clerk to validate the purchase.

Store verifies defect claim
The clerk inspects the physical part or reviews digital evidence (e.g., photos or a description if the request is online).

System checks inventory
The system confirms whether an identical or equivalent replacement part is currently in stock.

Replacement is issued
If the replacement item is available, the clerk initiates the replacement transaction and provides the new part to the customer.

Confirmation provided
The system generates a replacement confirmation, such as a printed receipt or automatic email.
