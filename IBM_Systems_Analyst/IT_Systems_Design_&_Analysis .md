**Final Project: Digital Transformation Strategy for a Midsized Retailer**

This project is designed to immerse you in a real-world application of systems analysis and business intelligence (BI) principles by developing a digital transformation strategy for a midsized retail chain. As a systems analyst, you will evaluate the retailer's current technology infrastructure, identify inefficiencies, and propose a modernized, cloud-based unified commerce platform to enhance operational efficiency, customer experience, and scalability. The project culminates in a strategic roadmap and actionable insights presented through structured reports and visual artifacts, aligning with the retailer's goal of seamless omnichannel operations and growth readiness.

The project has five steps, each building on the previous to create a comprehensive transformation strategy. This document provides detailed instructions, required information, and a project template. You will submit your project in PDF format. Your peers will grade it based on a provided rubric.

Objectives
  1. Assess existing systems to identify and document inefficiencies
  2. Identify and document functional and non-functional requirements for key stakeholders
  3. Evaluate alternative technology solutions using structured criteria to recommend the most suitable option.
  4. Assess feasibility and risks associated with proposed solutions to ensure practical implementation.
  5. Communicate findings and recommendations effectively

**Scenario**

You are a systems analyst at a midsized apparel and accessories retailer. The retail chain has approximately 50 stores and an e-commerce platform in the United States. The retailer faces significant challenges due to outdated point-of-sale (POS) systems (Windows CE), fragmented data across on-premises SQL databases for every store and Magento 1.9 for e-commerce, and a suboptimal online experience. These issues result in checkout delays, stockouts, inconsistent customer data, and lost sales.

The management seeks a digital transformation strategy to consolidate data, automate inventory processes, deliver a seamless omnichannel experience, and prepare for expansion to 100+ stores within the next two years.
